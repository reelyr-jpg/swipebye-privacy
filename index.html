SwipeBye Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

SwipeBye ("we," "our," or "the app") is built with your privacy as a core principle. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it's handled.

Data We Collect

None. SwipeBye does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. We do not use analytics services, advertising networks, crash reporting tools, or any third-party SDKs that gather information about you.

Photo Library Access

SwipeBye requests access to your photo library so it can display your photos for you to review. This access is used entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to any server, never shared with any third party, and never leave your iPhone.

When you choose to delete photos in the app, those deletions are handled by Apple's standard Photos framework. Deleted photos go to the "Recently Deleted" album in your Photos app for 30 days, where you can recover them if needed.

Data Storage

Any data SwipeBye saves about your progress (such as which photos you have reviewed) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. This data never leaves your device.

Children's Privacy

SwipeBye does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
